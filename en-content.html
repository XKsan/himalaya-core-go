CITY RETURN

The mountain is not far away.

City Return is a practice of awareness within the city.

Through walking, observation and stillness, we reconnect with our body, the city and everyday life.

What is City Return?

City Return is not a tour, a sightseeing route, or a check-in activity.

It is a way to experience the city again through attention, movement and presence.

A quiet return to your body, your surroundings and yourself.
