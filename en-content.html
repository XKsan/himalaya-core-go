City Return · Shanghai No.001

The mountain is not far away.

City Return is a real walking experience that takes place within the city.

We are not here to help you escape the city, and this is not a conventional City Walk. You move through Shanghai's streets, rivers, trees, architecture and everyday life, and gradually return your attention to your body, your surroundings and the present moment.

WHAT IS CITY RETURN

What is City Return?

City Return is not tourism, a course, or therapy.

It is a practice of awareness within the city. We begin with the ground beneath your feet, with walking, and with whatever state you are actually in when you arrive.

Each experience usually lasts around three hours. There is no task you have to complete, no standard answer, and no prior knowledge required. You simply arrive, slow down and walk.

Through history, rivers, neighbourhoods, green spaces and everyday life, we create a temporary distance from constant information and stimulation. Attention can return to the body, the environment and the present moment.
